**RentoX** is a classifieds platform for renting items out and renting items from others in Azerbaijan. Our goal is to create a simple and reliable place where people can quickly and affordably rent what they need without buying it, and where people with unused items can earn from them.

## What we do

- **For renters** we help you find the item you need quickly by category, city and price.
- **For lessors** we let you post listings for free or on favourable terms, open a store and promote your listings.
- **Safety:** listings are moderated, reports and support are in place, and sign-in uses a one-time code.

## Our principles

- **Transparency:** prices and rules are shown openly.
- **Trust:** we fight fraud and take reports seriously.
- **Convenience:** the website and mobile app are available in three languages (Azerbaijani, Russian, English).

## Important note

RentoX is a classifieds platform: it is not a party to the rental agreement and does not take rental payments. Rental terms are agreed directly between the lessor and the renter. For safe renting, read the "Rules and safety" section.
