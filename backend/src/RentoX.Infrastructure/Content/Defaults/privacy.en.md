## 2. What data we collect

**Data you provide**
- phone number (for registration and sign-in);
- name, profile photo, "about me" text, your chosen language;
- your listings: title, description, price, photos, location (city and district), contact details;
- store profile details (name, logo, cover image, description, contact details);
- conversations with other users and the photos you send;
- support requests and reports;
- listings you add to favourites and stores you follow.

**Data created while you use the platform**
- wallet transactions (top-ups, service fees, date and amount);
- notifications and your notification settings;
- a device identifier (token) for push notifications;
- technical data: IP address, device and browser type, time of operations, error logs, sign-in sessions;
- action records kept for security (for example, a change to a listing's status).

**RentoX does not store payment card details.** If card payment is introduced in the future, the payment provider will process the card details and we will receive only the result of the transaction.

## 3. What we use the data for

- creating your account, identifying you and keeping sign-in secure (one-time SMS code);
- posting, displaying and searching listings and contact between users;
- running the wallet and paid services;
- sending notifications (listing status, messages, support replies, etc.);
- handling support requests and reports;
- preventing fraud, spam and violations, and keeping the platform secure and working;
- meeting legal requirements and protecting our rights.

## 4. Legal grounds

We process data on the basis of your consent (given when you register), performance of the user agreement we conclude with you, compliance with our legal obligations, and our legitimate interest in keeping the platform secure.

## 5. Who can see the data

- **Other users:** the name, profile photo, content and photos of a listing, the listing owner's contact number, and the store profile. Only the participants can see a conversation.
- **Moderators and support staff:** data needed to run the platform, handle reports and keep it secure. These staff members' actions are recorded.
- **Third-party service providers (see below).**
- **Public authorities:** only in cases set out by law and on a justified request.

**We do not sell your personal data and do not give it to third parties for advertising.**

## 6. Third-party services

To operate the platform we use the following services; they receive only the data needed for their function:

- **SMS provider** — your phone number and the text of the code are passed on to send the sign-in code;
- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** — the device token and notification text are passed on to deliver push notifications; this service's servers may be outside Azerbaijan;
- **Payment gateway (in the future)** — when card payment is introduced, the provider will process payment details;
- **Hosting and infrastructure services** — to store data.

These providers have their own privacy rules. Data is transferred outside Azerbaijan only for the purposes above and in accordance with applicable law.

## 7. How long we keep data

- While your account is active we keep your profile, listing and conversation data.
- When you delete your account your personal data is anonymised (name, photo and "about me" text are removed; the account remains as a "deleted user" so that other users' conversation history is not broken).
- Financial transactions, security records and data that may be needed in a dispute are kept for the period required by law or needed to protect our rights, and are then deleted or anonymised.
- Technical logs are kept for a limited time.

## 8. Security

We take technical and organisational measures to protect your data: an encrypted connection (HTTPS), sign-in with a one-time code (passwords are not stored), sign-in sessions with a limited lifetime, restricted access to data, logging of staff actions, and the ability to block an account. No system is completely secure; do not share your code with anyone. RentoX staff never ask for a code or card details.

- object to processing and complain to the competent authority in accordance with the law.

To exercise your rights write to {{email}}. We may ask for additional information to confirm your identity. We reply within the period set by law.

## 10. Children

The platform is not intended for people under 16. If we find that data of a person under 16 has been collected, we will close the account and delete the data. Users aged 16–18 may use the platform only with the consent of a legal representative.
