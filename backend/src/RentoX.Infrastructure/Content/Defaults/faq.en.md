## How do I register?

Enter your phone number, then the one-time code from the SMS, and give your name. No password is needed. By registering you agree to the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy.

## The SMS code does not arrive. What should I do?

Check that the number is correct, wait a few minutes and press "Resend". If it still does not arrive, contact support. Never share the code with anyone.

## How do I post a listing?

Press "New listing", choose the category, add a title, description, price, location and photos, and submit. A moderator checks the listing; you will get a notification with the result.

## Is posting a listing free?

A certain number of active listings is free. After the limit is used up, activating a new listing is charged. The current limit and prices are shown in "Wallet" and on the listing page.

## Why was my listing rejected?

The reason for rejection is shown in the notification and on the listing page. You can fix the listing and submit it again. See "Rules and safety" for the rules.

## My listing has expired. What should I do?

A listing that has expired is hidden. You can renew it in "My listings". You get a reminder before it expires.

## How do I top up my wallet?

In "Wallet" press "Top up". The balance is spent on activating listings, moving them up the list, and VIP.

## Can I get money back from my wallet?

No. The balance is not refundable or withdrawable; it is spent only on the platform's services. If an amount was wrongly charged or credited because of a technical error, contact support and it will be checked and corrected.

## What are "Move up" and VIP?

These are paid services. "Move up" lifts a listing to the top of the list for a period, and VIP shows it among specially featured listings. Prices and periods are shown in "Wallet" and on the listing page.

## Does RentoX handle rental payments?

No. RentoX is only a classifieds platform. Rental terms, price, deposit and payment are agreed directly between the lessor and the renter. Do not pay in advance without seeing the item.

## How do I open a store?

In your account go to "Store", fill in the details (name, logo, description, contacts) and submit for review. Once approved, your store appears on the site.

## I saw a fraudster or someone breaking the rules. What should I do?

Press "Report" on the listing or conversation page. Moderators handle reports. Never share the one-time code, card details or a password.

## How do I delete my account?

In your profile choose "Delete account". When it is deleted your personal data is anonymised. See the "Privacy Policy" for details.

## How do I contact support?

Create a request in the "Support" section of the platform or write to {{email}}. We usually reply within 5 business days.
