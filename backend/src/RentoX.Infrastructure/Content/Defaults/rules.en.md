This page explains how to post listings correctly, what content is prohibited, and how to rent safely. Following the rules protects both you and other users.

## 1. Rules for posting listings

- **The title and description must be clear and truthful:** the item's name, condition, main features and rental terms (the period unit and, if there is one, the deposit amount).
- **Photos must be real.** Upload photos of the item itself. Do not use photos taken from the internet or belonging to someone else without permission.
- **The price must be real and complete.** Do not write "price by agreement" or an unrealistic token price ("1 AZN").
- **Choose the right category and location.** A listing in the wrong category may be rejected.
- **One listing per item.** Posting the same listing again and again counts as spam.
- **You must have the item and the right to rent it out.** A listing for an item that does not exist or does not belong to you is fraud.
- Do not put links to other sites, payment links or misleading promises in the text of a listing.

## 2. Prohibited listings

It is prohibited to post a listing for renting out:

- firearms, ammunition, explosives and other weapons;
- narcotic and psychotropic substances and the means of making them;
- fake documents, counterfeit money, and stolen or illegally obtained items;
- items and services that by law require a licence, permit or special document (if you do not have one);
- human organs, live animals and products from protected species;
- dangerous equipment whose use is prohibited by law or which is technically dangerous to health;
- adult content, or content that is offensive, promotes hate or promotes violence;
- listings posted using another person's personal data or photos without permission;
- listings meant to deceive under the guise of renting, to collect advance payments or to extract money.

In doubtful cases a listing is checked by a moderator and may be rejected.

## 3. Tips for renting safely

**For renters**
- **Do not pay in advance** without seeing and checking the item. Pay after you have checked and received it.
- Arrange to meet, if possible, in a busy, well-lit place and tell people you know.
- Photograph or film the condition of the item at handover.
- Agree the deposit and rental terms in writing (amount, period, how it will be returned).
- Make sure the photos in the listing are real: ask to see the item live or to be sent extra photos.

**For lessors**
- Check the renter's identity (ID card) and write down their contact details.
- Take a deposit and draw up a handover act or a receipt.
- Photograph or film the condition of the item at handover and compare it when it is returned.
- Agree in writing in advance the price, period, and the rules for late return and damage.
- Hand valuable items only to someone you have checked.

**For everyone**
- **Never share the one-time code, card details or a password with anyone.** RentoX staff never ask for them.
- Anyone sending a payment link "from RentoX" or asking for a "confirmation code" is a fraudster. RentoX does not accept rental payments.
- A price that is too low, an urgent demand for payment, and insistence on moving the conversation off the platform are signs of fraud.
- If you see a suspicious listing or user, report it straight away.

## 4. Reports and sanctions

- If you see a violation, press the **"Report"** button on the listing or conversation page. Moderators check reports.
- A listing that breaks the rules may be rejected, hidden or deleted.
- For repeated or serious violations an account may be suspended temporarily or blocked permanently.
- If fraud is found, RentoX may, in cases provided by law, pass data to the competent authorities.

## 5. If a dispute arises

RentoX is not a party to the rental agreement and does not take payments; rental terms are agreed directly between the parties. If there is a dispute, first negotiate with the other party and keep the conversation and any evidence. If you believe your rights have been violated, you may apply to the competent authorities.
